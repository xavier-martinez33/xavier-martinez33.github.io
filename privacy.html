Privacy Policy

Hermes Personal Integration · Last updated: 4 October 2026

1. Scope

This policy covers a private, self-hosted software integration ("the Integration") operated by a single individual for their own personal use. The Integration is not offered, distributed, or made available to any other person, and has no users other than its operator.

2. What data is accessed

After the operator authorizes the Integration through Google's OAuth 2.0 consent screen, the Integration may access, on the operator's own Google Account only:

  • Google Calendar events (read and create)
  • Google Drive files in folders the operator has explicitly shared
  • Google Sheets and Google Docs created by or owned by the operator

3. How data is used

Data is used solely to carry out tasks the operator requests, such as creating a calendar reminder or reading a data file the operator has exported into their own Drive. Data is processed locally on hardware owned and operated by the operator.

4. Data sharing

No data is sold, rented, transferred, or disclosed to any third party. No data is used for advertising, profiling, or training machine-learning models. No analytics or tracking services are used by the Integration.

5. Data storage and retention

OAuth credentials are stored on the operator's own hardware with restricted file permissions. Data retrieved from Google is retained only as long as it is needed for the operator's own records, and can be deleted by the operator at any time. Nothing is stored on external servers.

6. Security

Authentication uses Google OAuth 2.0. The Integration never receives or stores the operator's Google password. Access can be revoked at any time by the operator via Google Account permissions, which immediately ends all access.

7. Children's privacy

The Integration is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone other than its single operator.

8. Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on the project repository.

This policy applies to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
