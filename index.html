Hermes Personal Integration

A private, single-user integration that lets one person (the account owner) access their own Google Calendar, Drive, Sheets and Docs from their own self-hosted assistant software.

What it does

  • Reads and creates events on the owner's own Google Calendar.
  • Reads files from folders the owner has explicitly shared with it.
  • Reads and writes the owner's own Google Sheets and Docs.

Who can use it

Nobody else. There is no sign-up, no user base, and no public distribution. The software runs on the owner's own hardware and authenticates only as the owner, using Google OAuth.

Data handling

Data is requested from Google, used locally on the owner's own equipment, and is not transmitted to or stored by any third party. See the See the privacy policy and terms of service.
